BorderHop

Privacy Policy, Terms of Use & Support — Effective Date: November 20, 2026

1. Overview

BorderHop ("the App") is a mobile geography and land-border route puzzle game for iOS and iPadOS. We respect your privacy and comply with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, the ePrivacy Directive, the IAB Europe Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF v2.2), and Apple App Store Guidelines.

2. Data We Collect & How It Is Used

  • Local Game Progress: Your completed levels, earned stars, coins, daily challenge streaks, visited countries (passport stamps), language, and sound preferences are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage (UserDefaults). We do not collect or store this data on our own servers.
  • Leaderboards (Apple Game Center): When you use the Leaderboard feature, your total stars are transmitted to Apple Game Center to display global rankings.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): To keep BorderHop free to play for non-subscribed users, we use Google AdMob for optional rewarded video ads. With your consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, and subject to Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework, Google and its certified advertising partners may collect and process device identifiers (Apple IDFA), coarse IP location, and ad interaction data to show personalized ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. Active subscribers do not see ads.
  • In-App Purchases & Subscriptions: Subscriptions are processed securely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We do not receive, process, or store your payment card details or Apple ID billing information.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): Used for personalized advertising, device identifier access (IDFA), and ad measurement via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP / CMP) and Apple's ATT prompt.
  • Contractual Necessity (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): Used to provide core game mechanics, save progress locally, manage subscriptions, and synchronize optional Apple Game Center leaderboards.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): Used for app stability, security, and fraud prevention.

4. Managing Consent & Your Rights (GDPR Art. 15 & 17)

  • Manage Tracking: You may change or withdraw your tracking consent at any time in your iOS device Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete All Local Data: Open Settings → Data & Progress → Reset Game Progress inside the App to permanently erase all saved levels, stars, coins, streaks, and passport stamps from your device. Uninstalling the App also removes all locally stored data.

5. Terms of Use & Auto-Renewable Subscriptions

BorderHop offers an optional auto-renewable monthly subscription (BorderHop Pro) that unlocks ad-free gameplay, unlimited in-game tips, and Custom Sandbox Mode.

  • Billing: Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase.
  • Auto-Renewal: Subscriptions automatically renew unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period.
  • Manage or Cancel: You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions or via Manage Subscription inside the App.
  • Standard EULA: Use of the App is also governed by Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.
